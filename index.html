Search Syntax Guide

You can use advanced operators to refine your oral history searches:

  • Exact Phrases: Wrap terms in quotes to search for exact sequences.
    Example: "black smith" or "lincoln high school"
  • OR Operator: Find records matching either term.
    Example: railroad OR brickyard
  • AND Operator: Find records containing all terms.
    Example: grocery AND delivery
  • Combination: Combine operators for precise queries.
    Example: school AND (elementary OR high)
